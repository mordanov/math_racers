# Math Racers

### Make math the way to win.

Math Racers turns arithmetic practice into a bright, energetic racing game for
children ages 6–12. Kids create a character they love, solve math challenges,
and cheer as their racer surges toward the finish. Correct answers help them
pull ahead; mistakes are just small setbacks on the way to the next race.

## A race for every kind of day

- **Quick Race:** Jump into a fast, friendly competition.
- **Championship:** Race through a season and aim for the top of the standings.
- **Duel:** Take on one rival in a close head-to-head race.
- **Training:** Practice at your own pace, with no timer or opponents.

## More than a finish line

Children can create their own animal racers, discover new personal bests, and
celebrate progress with achievements and rewards. The challenge grows with
their skills, while every race is designed to keep the focus on trying,
learning, and having fun—not getting everything right the first time.

For families, Math Racers makes it easier to turn “time to practice” into
“let’s race again.” Parents can guide what children work on and follow their
progress, while kids stay in the driver's seat.

**Learn a little. Race a lot. Come back proud.**
