# Visual Language

**Level:** Specification
**Status:** Authoritative
**Source:** art_bible.md Part I
**See also:** [character-design.md](character-design.md), [ui-style.md](ui-style.md)

---

## Design Principle

> **"Pixar-quality warmth with Nintendo-level readability."**

Children should immediately perceive the world as: safe, cheerful, colourful, energetic, and optimistic.

Nothing should appear realistic, frightening, or sarcastic.

---

## Emotional Goals

**Primary:** Joy, Curiosity, Energy, Confidence, Safety, Wonder

**Secondary:** Excitement, Surprise, Pride, Discovery, Friendship

Negative emotions must never dominate any screen.

---

## Visual Keywords

`playful` · `rounded` · `expressive` · `colourful` · `premium` · `polished`  
`soft` · `energetic` · `optimistic` · `readable` · `approachable` · `magical`

When a design decision is uncertain, these words resolve it.

---

## Visual Style

**Stylised 3D Illustration** — images look like professionally rendered 3D animation from a feature-length animated film.

Not: flat illustration, anime, comic book, or realistic painting.

---

## Shape Language

Preferred: circles, rounded rectangles, soft curves, smooth silhouettes.

Avoid: sharp triangles, thin spikes, hard edges, aggressive geometry.

Children associate rounded shapes with safety.

---

## Silhouette Rule

Every important object must be recognisable from its silhouette alone, without colour or texture.

If removing colour makes an object unidentifiable, redesign it.

---

## Primary Palette

`Sky Blue` · `Golden Yellow` · `Grass Green` · `Coral Orange` · `Warm Red` · `Soft Purple` · `Turquoise` · `White`

---

## Secondary Palette

`Mint` · `Peach` · `Lavender` · `Cream` · `Sand` · `Light Teal` · `Warm Brown`

---

## Neutral Colours

Neutrals must remain warm. Avoid cold grey interfaces.

`Warm White` · `Light Cream` · `Soft Beige` · `Light Stone` · `Warm Grey` · `Dark Charcoal`

---

## Colour Usage Rule

```
70%  neutral
20%  primary colour
10%  accent
```

Accent colours should attract attention only to important interactions.

---

## Colour Semantics

| State | Colour |
|-------|--------|
| Correct answer | Bright Green |
| Incorrect answer | Warm Orange |
| Selected element | Blue Glow |
| Disabled element | Soft Grey |

Never rely on colour alone — always combine with icons, animation, or movement.

---

## Lighting

Warm, sunny, soft — global illumination with subtle rim light. Never dark scenes or harsh spotlights.

---

## Shadows

Soft, slightly blurred, subtle. No hard black shadows. Shadows create depth, not drama.

---

## Visual Hierarchy

```
Characters → Mathematics → Interaction → Animation → Environment → Decorations
```

Backgrounds must never compete with gameplay elements.

---

## Visual Density

The interface must breathe. Empty space is valuable. Children should identify interactive elements within 1 second.

---

## Colour Contrast & Accessibility

Must support colour blindness — combine colour with icons and motion.

Use large shapes, strong contrast, and simple silhouettes.

---

## Consistency

Every asset must appear to belong to the same universe. A new illustration should be indistinguishable from launch-day artwork.

Consistency is more important than individual creativity.
