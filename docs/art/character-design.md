# Character Design Rules

**Level:** Specification
**Status:** Authoritative
**Source:** art_bible.md Part II
**See also:** [visual-language.md](visual-language.md), [../prompts/gpt-image-prompts.md](../prompts/gpt-image-prompts.md)

---

## Design Philosophy

Characters must immediately communicate: kindness, confidence, optimism, curiosity, energy.

The goal is emotional connection, not realism.

No avatar should look aggressive or intimidating.

---

## Character Pillars

Every avatar must be: memorable, readable, expressive, charming, athletic, unique, child-friendly.

---

## Universal Anatomy

```
Large head → Expressive face → Compact torso → Long legs → Medium arms → Large hands → Large feet
```

**Proportions:**
- Head ≈ 40% of total height
- Torso ≈ 25%
- Legs ≈ 35%

The large head maximises emotional expression.

---

## Eyes

- Large, bright, expressive, symmetrical, high contrast.
- Never tiny or realistic.
- Eyes are the most important feature.

## Eyebrows

Rounded, soft, flexible. Never sharp or angular.

## Nose

Simplified: rounded button, small animal nose, or soft triangle. Never realistic anatomy.

## Mouth

Wide smiles. Neutral expressions remain friendly. No sharp teeth visible.

---

## Ears by Species

| Species | Ear Shape |
|---------|-----------|
| Fox | Tall triangular |
| Rabbit | Long floppy |
| Bear | Rounded |
| Cat | Soft pointed |
| Mouse | Large circular |

Ears contribute strongly to silhouette recognition.

---

## Hair

Stylised: simplified shapes, soft curves, readable volume.  
Avoid realistic strands, highly detailed styles, or sharp spikes.  
Must remain recognisable at thumbnail size.

## Fur

Smooth, soft, simplified. Avoid highly detailed rendering. Illusion of softness over individual hair detail.

## Skin

All tones receive equal artistic quality. No tone should appear more saturated or less polished than another.

---

## Colour Harmony per Avatar

```
Main colour    60%
Accent colour  25%
Detail colour  10%
Neutral          5%
```

Every avatar must read clearly in its silhouette with 3 colours maximum.

---

## Clothing

- Sports-focused: running shorts, athletic tops, trainers.
- Bright, primary colours.
- Simplified shapes with minimal detail.
- Species-specific accessories enhance personality (tail holes, ear openings, etc.).

---

## Accessories

Optional, child-safe only:
- Headbands, caps, scarves
- Glasses (simplified round shapes)
- Backpacks (small, stylised)
- No sharp objects, weapons, or realistic items.

---

## Expressions Library (minimum 10)

| Expression | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Happy | Wide smile, raised cheeks, sparkling eyes |
| Excited | Open mouth, wide eyes, leaning forward |
| Thinking | Hand on chin, eyes looking up |
| Focused | Determined brow, set jaw, forward lean |
| Surprised | Round eyes, open mouth, raised eyebrows |
| Celebrating | Arms raised, jumping, confetti eyes |
| Tired | Drooping eyes, slight slump |
| Encouragement | Thumbs up, warm smile |
| Neutral | Relaxed, friendly baseline expression |
| Confused | Tilted head, one raised eyebrow |

---

## Forbidden Characteristics

- Realistic anatomy or body proportions
- Aggressive, angry, or threatening expressions
- Mature clothing or accessories
- Weapons or dangerous objects
- Existing franchise characters or recognisable real people
- Political or religious symbols
- Any characteristic that could frighten a young child

---

## Consistency Across Versions

Characters must remain recognisable across portrait versions and poses.

Consistent elements: face, colours, clothing, accessories, proportions.

Changing poses must not change identity.

---

## Success Criteria

A character design is successful when:
- A child instantly recognises their avatar in a race thumbnail.
- The character looks like it belongs in a premium animated film.
- A new species can be added without updating any existing art rules.
