# Vision — Math Racers

**Level:** Vision
**Status:** Authoritative
**Source:** GDD Chapter 1, Chapter 14

---

## One-Line Summary

Math Racers is a premium educational racing game where children practise arithmetic by racing AI-generated animal avatars across a colourful track.

---

## The Core Loop

```
Child Solves an Arithmetic Problem
              ↓
         Avatar Moves
              ↓
         Race Finishes
              ↓
       Child Feels Proud
              ↓
         Race Again
```

Mathematics is the engine. Racing is the experience.

---

## Three Pillars

| Pillar | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Learning** | Every interaction improves arithmetic fluency |
| **Creativity** | Children design unique AI-generated characters |
| **Joy** | Every moment — win or lose — should make the child smile |

No pillar may be sacrificed for another.

---

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Children aged 6–12 with basic arithmetic knowledge.

**Secondary:** Parents who want a safe, ad-free tool that measurably improves mathematics.

---

## Platform

Browser-based (desktop, laptop, tablet). No installation required.

---

## Design Promises

1. **Celebrates effort, never punishes mistakes.** Incorrect answers slow the runner — they never eliminate the player.
2. **No pay-to-win.** All gameplay-affecting systems are free.
3. **No manipulative retention mechanics.** No daily-login streaks, premium currencies, or FOMO.
4. **Children are creators, not consumers.** They build characters, not buy them.
5. **Complexity is invisible.** Adaptive AI, prompt engineering, and asset pipelines all serve one outcome: a joyful, responsive game.

## Long-Term Vision

Math Racers evolves from a mathematics game into a full educational platform supporting multiple subjects while preserving the same joyful racing experience.

```
v1.0  — Mathematics, single player
v1.5  — Content expansion (stadiums, species)
v2.0  — Social features (family profiles, replay)
v3.0  — AI-assisted tutoring, adaptive learning plans
v4.0+ — Additional subjects (Spelling, Geography, Science)
```

---

## Success Definition

The game has succeeded when a child says:

> "I'm much faster than I was last month!"

...instead of:

> "I need more points."
